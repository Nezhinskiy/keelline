`keelline uninstall [--dry-run] [--force PATH]` takes back what `init` and `upgrade` wrote:
files whose bytes are still Keelline's go, Keelline's sections come out of files that hold
other text, the ledger goes, and every file you edited stays and is listed. It refuses
while `.keelline/local/` holds notes or edited files that its ignore block keeps out of
git.
