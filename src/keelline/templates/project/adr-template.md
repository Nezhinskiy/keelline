# 0000. Template

The four-heading form Michael Nygard described: one decision per file, numbered, kept as
written once it has a status. Copy this file to `<next number>-<short-title>.md` and fill it
in; do not edit this one, and do not rewrite a decision after the fact — supersede it with a
new record that links back.

## Context

What forced the decision. The constraints, the measurements, and what was not known.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice: "we will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this costs.

## Status

Proposed, accepted, or superseded by a later record — name it.
